McDonald's makes more money from rent than from burgers. Every restaurant is a property deal with a drive thru attached. About 95% of McDonald's restaurants aren't run by McDonald's. Franchisees run them. And those operators pay the company two things: a royalty on sales and rent. That's the real business model. The company buys the land, owns the building, and leases it back to the person flipping the burgers. Rent and royalties. That's where the profit lives, not the food. And here's the clever part. Those payments come off the top. The operator pays whether the location thrives or struggles before they see a single cent of profit. So next time you see the golden arches, count the property, not the fries. Are you walking into a restaurant or a landlord's office? Tell me in the comments.
