He Sold 50 Shirts Without Owning One!

[0s – 8s] SCENE 1 (hook)
Two friends. One hundred dollars each. Same product. Very different strategy.

[8s – 19s] SCENE 2 (inventory)
Alex spent all his money buying five T-shirts for twenty dollars each. He sold them for thirty. That's fifty dollars gross margin.

[19s – 33s] SCENE 3 (preorder)
Maya did the opposite. She showed buyers a sample and collected fifty paid preorders. Only after confirming demand did she place the production order.

[33s – 46s] SCENE 4 (math)
Fifty orders at thirty dollars brought in fifteen hundred dollars. Production cost one thousand. That leaves five hundred dollars gross margin, before sample costs, shipping, fees, and refunds.

[46s – 58s] SCENE 5 (compare)
Same starting cash. Ten times the shirts sold. Preorders reduced inventory risk, but finding fifty buyers was the hard part. Which approach would you choose?
